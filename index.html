01 — Craft

Watch a screen get built.

Every interface we ship goes through the same six passes. Scroll, and the invoice screen on the right goes through them too.

  1. 01 / Structure

    Boxes before beauty.

    What goes where, and why. The layout comes from what you need to do on the screen, not from what looks busy.

  2. 02 / Type

    Hierarchy you read at a glance.

    One typeface, a strict scale, real words. The amount due is the loudest thing on the screen because it is the thing that matters.

  3. 03 / Colour

    Colour that means something.

    One accent for action, one signal for status. Nothing is coloured just to decorate it.

  4. 04 / Depth

    Surfaces with weight.

    Radius, shadow and layering tell your eye what can be pressed and what sits behind it.

  5. 05 / Motion

    Feedback for every tap.

    The button answers, the switch slides, the total settles. Motion explains what happened. It never shows off.

  6. 06 / Access

    Built for everyone who uses it.

    Keyboard focus, labels for screen readers, contrast measured against WCAG. I've delivered 508 compliance for clients — here it is measured live, not claimed.
